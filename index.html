    Workshop content · FY27 outline
    Source: MEOW Excel, Agenda tab

    Objective

    Equip Member Engagement Officers to lead engagement as a strategic driver of chapter health, member value and retention, using insight, experience design, inclusive community-building and data to deliver measurable outcomes.

    Modules · minutes
    How the 480 minutes are delivered

    Highest expected engagement

    • Member Journey & Experience Design
    • Chapter Health & Dashboard Challenge
    • Disengagement Intervention Lab
    • Forum, Peerdom & Belonging conversations
    • Member Panel / Peer Problem-Solving
    • Strategic Planning & 30-60-90 Execution
    Content notes
    Workshop success tips

    Lessons from past MEO workshops · Pietro

    What worked well

    • Practical engagement ideas and examples from other chapters.
    • A clearer view of the officer's role in onboarding, connection and retention.
    • Strong energy and peer sharing; people left with actionable concepts.

    What to improve

    • More concrete case studies and proven chapter practices, less theory.
    • More consistent facilitation; tailor examples to different chapter contexts.
    • More practical tools for disengagement, onboarding and member experience.

    Using data in the workshop

    • YPO is data rich: members, engagement, forums, events, learning, chapters.
    • Use dashboards, survey findings and benchmarks to bring content to life and prompt discussion.
    • Make sure participants know what data exists and how to access it during their year.
    • The management team coordinates with the Senior Director of Data & Insights where extra expertise helps.
